# Reflection

Day 1 helped me understand that Salesforce development starts
with understanding the business and designing the data model
before writing code.

I learned how to identify the main entities in a business
problem and represent them using Salesforce objects, fields,
and relationships.

The Placement Management System helped me understand how
different objects work together:

Student → Application → Job → Company

and how an Offer Letter can be connected to a selected
application.

One of the biggest lessons I learned was that a good data model
is the foundation of a good application. If the objects,
relationships, and fields are designed correctly, implementing
automation and business logic becomes much easier.

I also gained practical experience creating custom objects,
configuring fields, setting relationships, creating an app, and
managing navigation items.

## Key Takeaway

> Before writing code, understand the business and design the
> data correctly.

Day 1 gave me the foundation required for the upcoming work with
Flows, Validation Rules, Apex Triggers, and Apex Classes.
