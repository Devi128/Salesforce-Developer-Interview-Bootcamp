# Chapter 6 – Reflection

In this sprint, I learned how Apex Triggers help Salesforce respond automatically to business events.

I understood the difference between `before` and `after` triggers and learned why business logic should be kept inside Service classes instead of putting everything inside the Trigger.

I also implemented and tested automation where a Student's `Placed__c` field is automatically updated when an Application becomes `Selected`.

### Key Takeaway

> A good Trigger should be simple, reusable, and should respond to the right business event automatically.
