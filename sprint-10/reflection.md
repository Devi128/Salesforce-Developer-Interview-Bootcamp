# Reflection – Day 10

Day 10 helped me understand how different LWC components communicate with each other. I learned how to use `@api`, Custom Events, `@wire`, and `refreshApex()`.

I also learned how to create reusable components like `StatusBadge` and `ApplicationCard`. Connecting the LWC components with Apex helped me understand how the frontend and backend work together.

The most useful part was implementing the application flow where a new application automatically appears in **My Applications** without manually refreshing the page.

Overall, Day 10 improved my understanding of LWC component communication, reusable components, Apex integration, and building a more interactive Salesforce application.
